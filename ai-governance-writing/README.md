# AI Governance & Risk Writing

Published articles on Medium, written to make cyber and AI risk legible to non-technical stakeholders.

## Articles

### [Why I Think Every CISO Needs to Start Speaking in Dollars, Not Adjectives](https://medium.com/@emanukariuki/why-i-think-every-ciso-needs-to-start-speaking-in-dollars-not-adjectives-a1adf9337eb9)
*Published July 14*

Makes the case for Cyber Risk Quantification (CRQ) over qualitative "High/Medium/Low" risk labels, drawing on the FAIR model. Companion piece to the [Cyber Risk Quantification](../cyber-risk-quantification) project in this repository.

### [Your Organization Is Already Using AI. Does It Have a Governance Policy?](https://medium.com/@emanukariuki/your-organization-is-already-using-ai-does-it-have-a-governance-policy-dd3a1b0d8b17)
*Published August 11*

Follow-up piece prompted by a statistic from the CRQ article: 63% of organizations still lack an AI governance policy. Examines what a baseline AI governance policy should cover.

## Why this is here

Writing is part of how I work through GRC concepts before applying them technically, and part of how I show up publicly in the field. Both articles are cross-posted to [LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com/in/emmanuel-benard-kariuki).
